# Kerrsplat Publishing

Independent publishing imprint for books, photography, writing, and other creative work.

## About

Kerrsplat Publishing is the independent publishing imprint of Bill McNamee and Integrated Desktop Solutions, Inc., providing a dedicated identity for books, photography publications, zines, fiction, and other written and creative works.

The Kerrsplat name has roots in an earlier creative project. Kerrsplat.com was acquired in 2011 and developed as a platform for original writing by Bill McNamee along with work from other writers and artists. The original website was closed in 2017, with its creative content consolidated under BillMcNamee.com.

In 2026, Kerrsplat returned with a more defined purpose as a publishing imprint. While Bill McNamee | The Art of the Create remains the primary home for photography, writing, and other creative work, Kerrsplat serves as the publishing identity for projects developed for independent publication.

Initially focusing on work by Bill McNamee, Kerrsplat is intended to grow as a publishing home for selected work by other creators while maintaining an emphasis on independence, creative control, and care for the work being published.

## Scope

### Independent Publishing

Developing and publishing books, photography collections, zines, fiction, and other written and creative works through an independent publishing model.

### Photography & Visual Storytelling

Developing photography-based projects in which image selection, sequencing, presentation, and design contribute to the finished publication.

### Writing & Editorial Development

Developing original writing and editorial content for books and other publications, including project development, editing, and preparation for publication.

### Book Design & Production

Managing the creative and production process from concept and layout through image preparation, proofing, print production, and release.

### Creative Technology

Using digital publishing tools, creative workflows, and assistive and generative AI, where appropriate, to support editorial development, design, production, and publishing.

### Creative Independence

Maintaining editorial and creative control over the work published under the Kerrsplat name, with an emphasis on originality, transparency, and independence from outside commercial influence.

## Highlights

### 2011 | Kerrsplat.com

The Kerrsplat.com domain was acquired and developed as a creative platform showcasing original writing by Bill McNamee along with work from other writers and artists.

### 2016 | Off Season: Cape May, New Jersey

The first edition of *Off Season: Cape May, New Jersey* was independently published through Blurb, bringing together photography, book design, and independent publishing. The project predates Kerrsplat Publishing but represents an important foundation for its later development.

### 2017 | Kerrsplat Consolidation

The original Kerrsplat website was closed, with Bill McNamee's original writing and creative content consolidated under BillMcNamee.com.

### 2025 | The Art of the Create

BillMcNamee.com was relaunched as Bill McNamee | The Art of the Create, bringing photography, writing, independent publishing, creative technology, and selected work from across Bill McNamee's career together within a unified creative identity.

### 2026 | Kerrsplat Publishing

Kerrsplat Publishing was established as an independent publishing imprint through Bowker, formalizing current and future publishing activities under Bill McNamee and the Integrated Desktop Solutions, Inc. umbrella.

### 2026 | First Kerrsplat Publication

A revised edition of *Off Season: Cape May, New Jersey* entered development as the first planned publication under the Kerrsplat Publishing imprint.
